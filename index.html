ABOUT
MANU

I am Manu Díaz, a graphic designer interested in the way visual language can shape perception. My practice moves between identity, editorial composition, illustration, poster design and digital interfaces.

I like working with strong hierarchies, precise typography, clean structures and small unexpected details. Every project begins with a concept, but it only becomes real when image, rhythm and system start working together.
